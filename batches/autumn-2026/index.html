Programme

From discovery to a working product

Ten lab weeks, from October 2026 to January 2027, with breaks around the LBS calendar.

Provisional programme outline. Session dates, times and locations will be confirmed by the programme team.

Fall 2026 programme outline
Lab weekDatesFocus and tasksWorkshop / session
1 12–16 Oct 2026 Discover: problem, users and stakeholders Teams present their ideas
Break 19–23 Oct LBS block week —
2 26–30 Oct Validate: research, evidence and constraints AI Product Management
3 2–6 Nov Design: solution design and prototyping Build with AI: big picture & fundamentals
4 9–13 Nov Build: first working product and data access Build with AI: hands-on
5 16–20 Nov Build: working MVP and midpoint review Midpoint Session
6 23–27 Nov Test: at least 3 real users and collect test inputs —
Break 30 Nov–1 Jan LBS block week, then December break —
7 4–8 Jan 2027 Evaluate: test set, scores and failure log AI Evaluation & Testing
8 11–15 Jan Iterate: top fixes, rerun tests and final build —
9 18–22 Jan Demo: rehearse, gather feedback and fine-tune Presentation Skills and Showcase rehearsal
10 25–29 Jan Present: finished product and live demo Final presentation day
