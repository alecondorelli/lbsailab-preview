What participants learn in the Lab

Participants develop the product, technical, and leadership skills needed to build AI products from problem discovery to launch

Define the problem

Turn ambiguous workflows into clear product opportunities

Understand the user

Run interviews, map needs, and identify where AI can create value

Manage stakeholders

Work with participants, alumni, clubs, staff, and partners to navigate constraints and adoption

Build with AI

Use AI-assisted tools to prototype and ship quickly

Evaluate and iterate

Test quality, learn from feedback, and improve the product

Think toward product-market fit

Move from a promising demo to something people understand, adopt, and return to
