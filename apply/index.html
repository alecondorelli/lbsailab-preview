Spring 2027 application details

Who should join

You do not need to be a professional software engineer

You do need curiosity, discipline, and willingness to learn by shipping. The Lab is practical, team-based, and focused on the product loop.

Who this is for

  • LBS participants with a product idea
  • Operators who see a workflow that can be made better with AI
  • Future founders looking for co-builders and early users
  • Participants who want practical AI fluency beyond classroom learning

What you will do

  • Form or join a team
  • Choose an LBS workflow
  • Prototype with AI-assisted tools
  • Test with LBS participants and alumni
  • Improve based on feedback
  • Work with mentors
  • Demo what you build

What you leave with

  • A sharper problem statement
  • A working AI product prototype
  • Evidence from users
  • People to keep building with
